      Driving tips

      Driving assist (Settings) helps you steer and brakes for you before tight corners: Easy helps the most, Pro turns it off. These cars are fast but the stadium tracks are tight. Brake before hairpins: the 150, 100 and 50 boards tell you where. Too much speed in a corner and the car slides wide into the wall. The grass and wet patches have far less grip.

      Chevron pads and nitro give a boost. Oil spins you out, cones and tyre stacks slow you down, and hard crashes damage the car and cut its top speed. Coins come back every lap and pay for upgrades, new cars, paint, rims and neon in the Garage.
